How it works

Three steps to a really good deal.

Find, reserve, pick up. Less waste, and dinner is sorted.

  1. 01 🔍

    Find

    Open the app and see which cafés and shops near you have surprise bags tonight.

  2. 02 💳

    Reserve

    Pay in the app. The bag is kept for you, with a pickup time and a code.

  3. 03 🛍️

    Pick up

    Show the code at the counter, take your bag and enjoy a meal you saved.

Why it matters

Almost a fifth of all food in shops and homes is thrown away.

Every surprise bag is food that was grown, cooked and paid for, and was about to go in the bin. One bag rescued is one meal that is eaten instead.

💰 Rescue one bag a week and you save about 100 000 ₸ a year.

  • 1.05 bn tonnes of food were wasted in the world in 2022
  • 132 kg of food wasted per person every year
  • 60% of that waste happens at home
  • 8–10% of world greenhouse gas comes from lost and wasted food

Source: UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024.
