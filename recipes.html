Recipes · Leftover kitchen

Cook it, don't bin it.

More bread in your bag than you can eat? Six easy recipes for the things that are left in a surprise bag most often.

Recipe of the week

40 min · easy · from a bakery bag

Bread pudding with apples

Yesterday's bread, buns or croissants become a warm dessert. The drier the bread, the better it soaks up the milk.

How to cook
  1. Cut 300 g of bread into cubes and put them in a baking dish.
  2. Mix 2 eggs, 400 ml of milk, 2 spoons of sugar and cinnamon.
  3. Pour it over the bread and add one apple in thin slices.
  4. Bake at 180 °C for 30 minutes, until the top is golden.

15 min · easy · bakery bag

Garlic croutons

For soup or salad. Dry bread is perfect here.

How to cook
  1. Cut the bread into small cubes.
  2. Mix with oil, salt and a crushed garlic clove.
  3. Fry in a pan for 5–7 minutes, stirring.

20 min · easy · meals bag

Fried rice with anything

Rice, chicken or vegetables from a dinner bag get a second life.

How to cook
  1. Fry chopped vegetables and meat in a hot pan.
  2. Add cold rice and one egg, stir quickly.
  3. Finish with soy sauce and green onion.

5 min · very easy · grocery bag

Soft fruit smoothie

Bananas with brown spots are the sweetest.

How to cook
  1. Put 2 bananas and any berries in a blender.
  2. Add a glass of milk or yogurt.
  3. Blend for 30 seconds and drink it cold.

35 min · easy · fruit and veg box

Vegetable soup

Carrots, potatoes and peppers that will not last until the weekend.

How to cook
  1. Fry onion and carrot in a pot for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the other vegetables and 1.5 l of water.
  3. Cook for 25 minutes, add salt, pepper and herbs.

10 min · very easy · sweets bag

Cake in a glass

Three pieces of different cakes make one beautiful dessert.

How to cook
  1. Break the cake into pieces.
  2. Put layers of cake, yogurt and berries in a glass.
  3. Keep it in the fridge for 15 minutes.

Keep it fresh

How long does it last?

Four simple rules for the food from your bag.

  • 🥐

    Bread and pastry

    2 days in a bag, or slice it and freeze it for a month.

  • 🥗

    Salads and meals

    Eat them the same evening or keep them in the fridge until tomorrow.

  • 🍰

    Cakes

    In the fridge, in a closed box, for up to 2 days.

  • 🍎

    Fruit

    Keep bananas away from other fruit: they make it ripe faster.
