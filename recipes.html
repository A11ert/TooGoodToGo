Recipes

Three easy ideas for the food from your bag.

Bread pudding

Old bread, 2 eggs, milk and sugar. Bake for 30 minutes at 180 °C.

Fried rice

Fry rice with an egg and any vegetables or meat from your bag.

Vegetable soup

Cook the vegetables in water for 25 minutes. Add salt and herbs.
