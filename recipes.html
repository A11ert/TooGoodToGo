How long does it last?

Four simple rules for the food from your bag.

  • 🥐

    Bread and pastry

    2 days in a bag, or slice it and freeze it for a month.

  • 🥗

    Salads and meals

    Eat them the same evening or keep them in the fridge until tomorrow.

  • 🍰

    Cakes

    In the fridge, in a closed box, for up to 2 days.

  • 🍎

    Fruit

    Keep bananas away from other fruit: they make it ripe faster.
